Questions

Before you join.

Do I need to know how to edit?

Helps, but no. A full clipping course is built into the Whop that walks you through exactly how we cut clips that perform, plus the templates and style guide we use on real campaigns. If you can follow a tutorial and put in reps, you can do this.

Do I need to show my face?

No. You're clipping existing footage from our brands and creators. You never have to film yourself or be on camera.

What do I need to get started?

An editing setup you're comfortable with, a phone or computer to post from, and the willingness to put in volume. Everything else is inside.

How and when do I get paid?

You're paid based on the views your clips generate, tracked per campaign and paid out on a set schedule. Full terms are in the editor handbook once you're in.
